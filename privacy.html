Privacy policy

Home Command is a personal app used only by the household that runs it.

What it accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, Home Command uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read and change the owner's own Nest thermostat: temperature, humidity, mode, set points, eco and fan.

Where the data stays

The sign-in token and thermostat readings are kept only on the household's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent anywhere except to Google to carry out the owner's requests.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect at any time in Home Command's settings, or remove access from their Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
